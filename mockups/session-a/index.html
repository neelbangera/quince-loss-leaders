    The source

    United States catalog, in US dollars. Costs are the breakdowns Quince publishes on its own product pages.

    What a style is

    One product at one price. Colours and sizes that share a price are counted together.

    What this is not

    A difference against disclosed cost, not Quince’s profit or loss. Marketing, returns and overhead are not in it.
